# MafiaBoy DDoS Attack – Project Report

## 1. Introduction

Cybersecurity refers to the protection of computers, networks, servers, and data from digital threats. In today's world, most businesses and organisations depend on the internet, making network security very important.

One of the major threats to online services is a Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attack. The MafiaBoy attack of February 2000 is a famous example of this type of cyber incident.

## 2. What is a DDoS Attack?

DDoS stands for Distributed Denial of Service.

It is a type of cyberattack in which multiple devices send a large amount of traffic towards a website or server. This can overload the system and make the service unavailable to genuine users.

The main purpose of such an attack is to interrupt the normal functioning of an online service.

## 3. Background of the MafiaBoy Attack

MafiaBoy was the online nickname of Michael Calce, a teenager from Montreal, Canada.

In February 2000, several popular websites experienced major service disruptions due to DDoS attacks associated with him.

Some of the affected websites included Yahoo, Amazon, eBay, and CNN.

This incident received worldwide attention and highlighted the importance of internet security.

## 5. Impact of the Attack

The MafiaBoy incident affected several major online services.

Its major impacts included:

- Temporary unavailability of websites.
- Disruption of online services.
- Financial losses for businesses.
- Inconvenience for internet users.
- Increased awareness of cybersecurity risks.

## 6. Prevention Methods

Organisations can use several security measures to reduce the risk of DDoS attacks.

1. Firewalls: Help filter unwanted network traffic.
2. Traffic Monitoring: Helps identify unusual traffic patterns.
3. Rate Limiting: Controls the number of requests accepted by a service.
4. Load Balancing: Distributes incoming traffic across multiple servers.
5. DDoS Protection Services: Help detect and filter malicious traffic.

## 7. Conclusion

The MafiaBoy DDoS attack was an important event in cybersecurity history. It demonstrated how online services can be affected by excessive malicious traffic.

This incident teaches us that organisations must continuously improve their network security and use proper protection mechanisms to keep their services available.
